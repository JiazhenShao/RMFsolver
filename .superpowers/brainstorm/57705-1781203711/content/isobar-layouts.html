How should the constant-pressure trajectory be shown?

All three use the older red-curve, Computer-Modern-inspired style. Click the design that best supports the Methods derivation.

A. Single isobar

Cleanest PRC figure. Shows the path from equilibrated matter to the cold endpoint, but does not visually test the quadratic expansion.

B. Exact vs. quadratic

Recommended. The exact fixed-\(\mu_B\) isobar is overlaid with the expansion that produces the analytical relation. One panel, direct evidence, modest space.

C. Two-panel derivation

Most pedagogical: the second panel makes \(T^2\) linear in \(a^2\). Strongest explanation, but larger and slightly heavy for a Methods subsection.
