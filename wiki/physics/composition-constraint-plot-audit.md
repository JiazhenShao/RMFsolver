# Composition-constraint plot audit

The PI's neutral and revised simple screenshots agree with the exact massless, zero-temperature EOS, using local $a=n_K/n_B$, $n_K=(n_d-n_s)/2$, $n_B=(n_u+n_d+n_s)/3$, $\mu_K=\mu_d-\mu_s$ and $\mu_B=\mu_u+\mu_d+\mu_s=1200$ MeV. The original simple screenshot disagreed with the exact $\mu_Q=0$ model; the revised screenshot supplied on 2026-10-01 resolves that discrepancy. See [[quark-matter-eos]] for the reason neutrality was adopted and [[charge-neutral-quark-eos]] for the broader EOS derivation.

The email does not specify masses or temperature. Its statement $n_s+n_d=n_u$ omits the factor two required by quark-only electrical neutrality; the plotted neutral curve is consistent with $n_s+n_d=2n_u$. The email is not sufficient evidence that the same typo is in the plotting code.

## Exact curves

For massless flavors, $n_i=\mu_i^3/\pi^2+\mu_iT^2$. At $T=0$, neutrality gives $n_u=n_B$, $n_d=n_B(1+a)$, $n_s=n_B(1-a)$, hence

$$\frac{\mu_K}{\mu_B}=\frac{(1+a)^{1/3}-(1-a)^{1/3}}{1+(1+a)^{1/3}+(1-a)^{1/3}}.$$

This exact parameterization covers $0\le a\le1$ for nonnegative net flavor densities. Its endpoint is

$$\mu_K(a=1)=\frac{2^{1/3}\mu_B}{1+2^{1/3}}=669.007999\;\mathrm{MeV},\qquad \mu_Q=196.488001\;\mathrm{MeV}.$$

The endpoint has $(\mu_u,\mu_d,\mu_s)=(530.992001,669.007999,0)$ MeV and $n_u:n_d:n_s=1:2:0$. The approach flattens because $1-a=(\mu_s/\mu_u)^3$; it does not require infinite $\mu_K$. Continuing past it with the massless net-density formula gives net antistrangeness.

The alternative $\mu_d+\mu_s=2\mu_u$ is exactly $\mu_Q=0$ in this basis. With $\mu_q=\mu_B/3$,

$$a(\mu_K,0)=\frac{3\mu_q^2\mu_K/2+\mu_K^3/8}{\mu_q^3+\mu_q\mu_K^2/2}.$$

It reaches $a=1$ at $344.127767$ MeV, with $n_u:n_d:n_s=1:2.925186876:0.185037375$. Its zero-strangeness endpoint instead has $\mu_K=800$ MeV, $n_u:n_d:n_s=1:8:0$ and $a=4/3$. For either EOS, $a=1$ alone means $n_d=2n_u+5n_s$; adding neutrality is what forces $n_s=0$.

Superseded screenshot discrepancy: the original simple screenshot shows about $1.42$ near $500$ MeV and crosses $1$ near $240$ MeV. Its exact cause cannot be identified without the plotting source. The two exact curves must also have the same initial slope $9/(2\mu_B)$, unlike the original supplied pair. Finite $T$ cannot explain the larger original simple curve: for massless quarks its $a$ decreases with $T$ throughout this horizontal range. The revised simple screenshot instead follows the exact curve, including $a(500)=1.18969$ and the crossing near $344.13$ MeV.

Both exact $a=1$ endpoints exceed the small-imbalance regime: $\mu_K/\mu_q=1.6725$ (neutral) and $0.8603$ (simple). This invalidates the small-$\mu_K/\mu_q$ expansion at the endpoints; it does not by itself make the exact EOS states unphysical. These fixed-$\mu_B$ plots are not conserved-flux front trajectories.

## Artifacts and checks

Reproduction script, PNG/PDF comparison plots, digitized screenshot samples, numerical curves, finite-temperature samples, Wolfram input and full derivation are under `local_analysis_results/26-10-01/composition_constraints/`.
The independent neutrality root calculation matches the live `SQMsolver._quark_uds_state` fraction to $1.23\times10^{-15}$ over 28 states spanning $T=0,0.5,10,50$ MeV. Wolfram independently confirms the formula and endpoint roots. Both figures were visually inspected; screenshot markers are approximate pixel measurements, not the PI's numerical data.

The revised simple comparison is generated by `plot_revised_simple.py` in the same directory, using ten independently digitized points saved in `revised_simple_screenshot_samples.csv`. Their maximum absolute difference from the exact curve is $0.00845$, consistent with the low-resolution screenshot calibration. `simple_revised_comparison.png` and `.pdf` retain the original comparison style and show the resolved agreement; the PNG was visually inspected. The original screenshot samples and comparison figures are preserved as audit history.
